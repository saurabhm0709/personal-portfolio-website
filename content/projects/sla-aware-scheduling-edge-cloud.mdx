## Problem

In the edge-cloud continuum, applications run on a mix of resource-constrained edge nodes and larger cloud resources. Their behaviour (load, resource usage, latency) changes over time. When a scheduler only reacts once a service-level agreement (SLA) has already been violated, the violation has already happened by the time any corrective action takes effect.

**Question:** can predicting application behaviour ahead of time let a scheduler act _before_ SLA violations occur, and does that measurably reduce them compared to a reactive approach?

## Prediction

Application behaviour is modelled as a time series. Two families of models are being explored:

- **ARIMA**: a classical statistical model; interpretable and cheap to fit, suited to series with linear structure.
- **LSTM**: a recurrent neural network that can capture non-linear temporal patterns, at the cost of more data and tuning.
